How Did a Farmer's Phone Number on an Exam Question Inspire a Student's Dream?

1. What started with a tricky exam question

Sometimes the smallest details lead to the biggest surprises. On a Chinese high school English exam, a reading passage described a real strawberry advertisement — and included an actual phone number. A curious student decided to find out if the number was real, and what happened next was truly heart-warming.

2. A strawberry farmer who answered every call

The number belonged to a strawberry grower named Mr. Wang in Beijing, who had been cultivating fruit for over twenty years. He had been receiving mysterious calls from students every year for more than a decade, but he never minded. Each caller was taking the same English exam that mentioned his ad, so they all had the same question: was the number real?

Mr. Wang always answered with the same simple advice: "Study hard, be good students, and once you graduate, come visit me in Beijing." It was a throwaway line meant to be encouraging — but one student took it seriously.

3. From exam paper to dinner table

That student was He Ziqi. Years later, after earning his place at Beijing's Central University of Finance and Economics, he reconnected with Mr. Wang and explained that he had followed the farmer's advice. Delighted by the news, Wang treated He to a dinner of Peking duck — where another sweet surprise unfolded. In a city of 21 million people, Wang discovered that He's daughter attended the very school next to the university campus. The coincidence brought both families together in laughter.

He Ziqi is now pursuing postgraduate studies in artificial intelligence — carrying forward the encouragement that began with a chance encounter on an exam paper.

Fun Fact

The exam question that started it all was designed to test reading comprehension — the kind of ordinary classroom exercise that students take every day. Yet its phone number became an unlikely bridge between two strangers, reminding us that kindness, even in small print, can grow into something wonderful. Sometimes destiny hides in the footnotes.
