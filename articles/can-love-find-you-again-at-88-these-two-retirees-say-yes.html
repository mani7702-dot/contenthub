Can Love Find You Again at 88? These Two Retirees Say Yes

A Chance Meeting Among the Barbells

Barbara Thompson and Fred Jenkins had each said a long goodbye. Barbara moved into Oscott Retirement Village in Birmingham, England, ten years ago after losing her husband to cancer. Fred, a former Army physical-training instructor, lost his wife three years ago. Both had quietly assumed that was the end of that chapter of life.

Then Barbara's friend asked her to come to the village gym for moral support. Fred — who has loved weights and exercise his whole life — started coaching them. The friend eventually stopped coming, but Barbara kept showing up every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. The friendship grew, and so did something more.

"We Were Lonely, and Then We Found Each Other"

Fred admits he never expected this. "When my wife passed away, I just thought that that's the end of the world now," he said. "I never ever thought in my wildest dreams that I would ever have another partner, and neither did Barbara. We were lonely and then we found each other… It's a whole new ball game."

Barbara, who turned 89 last week, remembers the joy that crept in first. "We laughed a lot and we joked a lot, and I started looking forward to going to the gym," she said.

Today the pair are inseparable. They work out three times a week, play darts and dominoes, garden together, and dance whenever music comes on. They recently took their first holiday as a couple.

Independence, With Company

Perhaps the sweetest part is how they have arranged their lives. Barbara and Fred have no plans to marry or move in together. They keep their own apartments and enjoy, as they put it, the best of both worlds — lots of time together, and their independence intact.

Barbara believes their story carries a simple message for anyone who feels the best days are behind them. "Sometimes it's hard. People live in the past, they've been married, they've lost a partner. It's easy to give up sometimes, isn't it?" Their answer, plainly, is no — it is never too late.

Fun Fact

The pair met at a village gym, but it was a shared love of movement that started it all. Fred is a former Army fitness instructor, and Barbara now happily keeps to a three-day-a-week workout routine — a reminder that staying active is not just good for the body, but a surprisingly good way to meet people.
