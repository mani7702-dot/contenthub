5 Things: Meghan Marks Co-Host Role on Junior MasterChef Australia

2026-10-273 min

5 Things About Meghan's Junior MasterChef Australia Adventure

Meghan Markle is trading royal duties for kitchen duties! The Duchess of Sussex will co-host the 2027 season of Junior MasterChef Australia, cooking up fun alongside chef Poh Ling Yeow on Network 10. Here's what we know:

1. She Already Guest-Judged Last Year

Meghan first appeared on MasterChef Australia as a guest judge in 2025 — that episode became the show's biggest Sunday audience in years. Now she's stepping up to co-host the Junior version, which is all about kids aged 9 to 12 showing off their cooking chops.

2. Her Kitchen Is All About Family

"I love cooking with my own kids," Meghan said in a statement. "Seeing what these young stars create in the kitchen is going to be a lot of fun!" She also hosts a Netflix lifestyle show called With Love, Meghan, where she shares recipes and home life tips.

3. Junior MasterChef Started in the UK

Junior MasterChef began as a British show on the BBC in the 1990s, then came back in 2010 with a fresh format. It's now made in more than a dozen countries, with young chefs competing in fun challenges like making pasta from scratch and whipping up perfect cupcakes.

4. She's Moving Back to the UK This Year

Meghan and Prince Harry are relocating from California to the UK after their kids finish school for the year. They first moved to California in 2020, but now they're heading back across the pond — just in time for Meghan's new hosting gig.

5. No Payment Needed

Australia's Network 10 confirmed that Meghan was not paid for her 2025 guest appearance, and it's expected the co-hosting role will follow the same charitable-spirit tradition.
